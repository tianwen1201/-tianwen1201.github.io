我的教学

我相信，学生学中文，是为了将来能和真实的人一起完成真实的事。所以从初级到高级，我总想办法让课堂任务“走出教室”：初级学生的期末口试，设在中文桌和老师家的客厅里；三年级学生用一学期探究一个自己关心的社会问题，期末向社区公开展示；高年级学生在模拟记者会上担任中文发言人，或者把成语故事拍成视频，讲给本地的华裔小朋友听。听众变了，学生对待语言的方式也会跟着变。

我的课堂以体演文化教学法（Performed Culture Approach）为基础，重视在具体情境中培养语言能力与行为文化的得体性，也重视为学生设计清晰的任务链和语言脚手架，让输入真正转化为输出。

→ RESEARCH

我的研究

课堂里发现的问题，常常会变成我的研究问题。比如，我的三年级学生读文章时总说“生词太多”，但我发现他们真正的困难是把读懂的部分连成全文的意思。为此，我设计了 AI 阅读教练 Chinese Reading Coach，让它追问学生、但不替学生回答。我在课堂上试用，根据学生的对话记录逐轮修改设计，这项工作后来发展成一项基于设计的研究，也是我博士论文的起点。我希望技术在我的课堂里只做一件事：让学生自己的理解被看见、被追问、被修改。

About

Tianwen Han is a Chinese language instructor in the Chinese Flagship Program at the University of Mississippi, where she is also pursuing a Ph.D. in Applied Linguistics. She holds a B.A. (Shanghai Normal University, 2019) and an M.A. (East China Normal University, 2022) in Teaching Chinese to Speakers of Other Languages, and has taught at UVA-in-Shanghai, the Middlebury Chinese School, Bowdoin College, and Williams College (Language Fellow, 2021–22).

She has taught Chinese at all levels from first through fourth year, including intensive beginning Chinese, intermediate and advanced integrated courses, Chinese for news and media, Chinese literature, Classical Chinese, one-on-one domain courses, and a special-topics course she developed, Social China. Grounded in the Performed Culture Approach, her teaching asks students to use Chinese with real people for real purposes, often for audiences beyond the classroom. She also designs AI-supported learning tools for her own students and studies them through design-based research, the starting point of her dissertation on AI-assisted Chinese reading.
